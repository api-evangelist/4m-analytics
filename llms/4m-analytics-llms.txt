# 4M Analytics

> 4M Analytics builds the "Google Maps of underground infrastructure" — a utility AI mapping platform that compiles millions of scattered public utility records into a single, validated subsurface map. An AI conflation engine, satellite imagery, computer-vision object detection, and geospatial experts identify known and unknown underground utilities so infrastructure teams can research utilities faster, cut excavation risk, and avoid conflicts during planning, pre-construction, and design. Founded 2019 (Austin, TX and Tel Aviv); backed by Insight Partners. This llms.txt is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public 4M surface — 4M publishes no first-party llms.txt.

## APIs

- [4M WFS API](https://help.4manalytics.com/en/collections/3720127-api-and-integrations): OGC Web Feature Service that streams 4M's validated subsurface utility data (line and point features) in real time into GIS clients such as ArcGIS and QGIS. Authenticated with an admin-provisioned API key generated inside the 4Map application; endpoint URL is provisioned per organization. No OpenAPI is published.
